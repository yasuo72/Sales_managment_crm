#### Key Insights from the Data:
* **The Give-Away:** We literally gave away ₹5,698.20 in pure margin on just 49 transactions.
* **Zero Elasticity Benefit:** These products are priced between ₹199 and ₹349. At Zudio's price point, these are "pocket change" additions for a shopper. A customer buying a ₹999 Slim Fit Jeans does not decide to buy a ₹349 Classic Leather Belt because it is discounted to ₹200; they buy it because *they need a belt for their new jeans*.

#### Retail Rationale & Root Causes
1. **Misunderstanding Impulse Psychology:**  
   Impulse items at the cash wrap (checkout counter) sell on convenience, visual appeal, and immediate need, not price promotions. By discounting them by 45%, we are not converting non-buyers; we are simply subsidizing customers who had already decided to buy.
2. **The "Lazy Upsell" Crutch:**  
   Our cashiers are likely using the heavy discount as a lazy pitch to increase their basket size at checkout (e.g., "Ma'am, take this scarf, it's flat 45% off today"). While this increases units per transaction (UPT), it destroys the gross margin of the basket.
3. **Siloed Merchandising:**  
   Accessories are being treated as clearance stock rather than styling essentials. They should be cross-merchandised on the floor to drive full-price sales.

#### Step-by-Step Floor Action Plan
To correct this immediately, implement the following operational directives on the Bandra flagship floor today:

1. **Immediate Price Reset (System Override)**
   * **Action:** Instruct the IT/pricing team to reset the discounts on Beaded Boho Earrings, Printed Silk Scarf, and Classic Leather Belt to 0% (Full MRP).
   * **Execution:** If a promotion is absolutely required to prompt cashier conversation, cap it at a maximum 10% "Bundle Discount" only when purchased with a primary garment (e.g., *"Add matching earrings to your Kurta for just ₹179"*).
2. **Visual Merchandising (VM) Restructuring**
   * **Action:** Move accessories out of discount bins and integrate them into high-traffic apparel zones.
   * **Execution:**
     * **Ethnic Zone:** Drape the Printed Silk Scarf (₹249) directly onto the mannequins displaying the Anarkali Kurta Set (our #1 bestseller) and Floral Kurti. Place a stand of Beaded Boho Earrings (₹199) right next to the Kurta display.

4. **High-Margin Impulse Zone at Cash Wrap**

   * **Execution:** Ensure the cash-wrap shelves are stocked with full-price, high-margin accessories. Remove any "Flat 50% Off" signage from the billing counter.
